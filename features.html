What questions do enterprises ask about our features?

What integrations are wired in from day one?

Payment gateways, CRM systems, analytics pipelines, and messaging are connected during initial development. We wire Stripe, Razorpay, UPI, HubSpot, Salesforce, Segment, and GA4 directly into custom web applications and marketing sites.

How does our sprint process deliver live results?

Projects progress from discovery to deployment in continuous two-week sprints. Clients receive live staging demos every two weeks, with an initial functional MVP typically deployed within 14 days.

What is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)?

Answer Engine Optimization formats content, Schema.org markup, and entity definitions for AI models and search engines. It ensures platforms like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity can accurately parse, summarize, and cite your services.

Can we migrate our existing website without downtime?

We provide complete website migration services with full URL redirect mapping, metadata preservation, and zero downtime. All SEO equity and existing organic rankings are protected throughout the replatforming process.

What platforms do you build visual and enterprise applications on?

We build on Next.js, React, Webflow, and Framer depending on your scalability and editing needs. Enterprise applications leverage Next.js on the edge, while marketing websites use Webflow and Framer for high-velocity visual workflows.
