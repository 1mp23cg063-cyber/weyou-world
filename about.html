About weYou World

The architecture of everything
wired, from deep logic to design.

✦

weYou World is the web, app, and design wing of weYou X. We specialize across three core pillars: Design (Web Design, Motion Design), Development (Webflow Development, Enterprise Development, Framer Development, Figma to Webflow, Website Migration Services), and Growth (CRO, SEO & AEO) for ambitious enterprises worldwide.

Wing & Mission

What is the mission and structure of weYou World?

weYou World is the web, app and design wing of weYou X, based in Bengaluru and building for clients worldwide. It designs and builds the websites and apps enterprises put in front of their customers. As part of the weYou X family alongside enVoy and LegalIndia.ai, we build sites and apps that arrive connected to payments, CRM and analytics. While enVoy provides customer communication across WhatsApp, voice and chat, weYou World builds the digital storefronts, web applications, and customer interfaces that organizations run on.

Three Pillars

What core disciplines do we deliver?

Design Systems & Motion Craft

weYou World delivers brand, interface and motion design, design systems, and accessibility. We create responsive UI and UX, interactive prototypes, and micro-interactions across mobile and desktop viewports.

Visual & Full-Stack Development

We engineer production-grade web solutions across Next.js, React, Webflow, and Framer. From high-velocity marketing pages with custom code extensions to full-stack enterprise web platforms, we integrate mission-critical business tools directly into the application from day one. This includes payment processing with Stripe, Razorpay, and UPI; customer relationship workflows in HubSpot and Salesforce; event streaming through Segment; and modern cloud deployments on Cloudflare and AWS.

Conversion & Search Growth

Every build is structured for commercial performance and discoverability. We implement data-driven Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO), checkout funnel tuning, and user journey analytics. Simultaneously, we execute technical Search Engine Optimization and Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), structuring data with Schema.org standards and llms.txt definitions so that both classical search engines and generative AI models can accurately index and cite your enterprise.

Process & Principles

How do we approach engineering and collaboration?

Iterative Two-Week Sprints

We work in two-week sprints with live demos. Every project begins with a discovery call to map the business logic, customer journeys, and integrations before any code. A typical MVP is delivered in about 14 days, followed by two-week sprint cycles with live demos.

Intellectual Property & Transparency

Upon final settlement of project invoices, the client owns all bespoke custom designs, copywriting, visual assets, and application code developed specifically for the client's project. weYou World retains ownership of its underlying frameworks and pre-existing code libraries.

Frequently Asked

Frequently asked questions about weYou World

Where is weYou World located and who do you serve?

weYou World is headquartered in Bengaluru, India, and builds websites, applications, and design systems for clients worldwide. We configure regional payment architectures, data compliance rules, and localized stacks tailored to each client's target market.

What is the relationship between weYou World and enVoy?

weYou World is the web, app, and design wing of weYou X, while enVoy is our sister conversational AI communication product. weYou World builds the websites and applications, and wires enVoy in whenever clients require automated WhatsApp, voice, or web chat communication.

Who owns the code and design assets after project delivery?

Upon final settlement of project invoices, the client owns all bespoke custom designs, copywriting, visual assets, and application code developed specifically for the client's project.

What is the typical timeline to launch an initial MVP?

A typical MVP takes about 14 days following the initial discovery call. After the MVP, engineering continues in two-week sprints with live demos.

What technologies and visual platforms does your team support?

Our team builds primarily with Next.js, React, Webflow, and Framer. We choose the platform based on your team's editing needs, supporting everything from visual CMS publishing to custom edge-rendered web applications.
