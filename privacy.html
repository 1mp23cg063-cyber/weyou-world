Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 · Last Updated: November 2026 · Bengaluru, Karnataka, India

1. Overview

weYou World is the web, application, and design wing of weYou X. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website (weyouworld.com), submit project inquiries, or engage us for design, development, and growth services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you request a free website audit, submit a contact form, or communicate with our team via email or WhatsApp. This includes:

  • Contact Details: Your name, business email address, phone number, and company name.
  • Project Information: Current website URL, technical requirements, platform preferences (Webflow, Framer, Next.js), and project scope.
  • Technical & Usage Data: Anonymized analytical parameters such as browser type, general geolocation (country/city), and referrers to improve site performance and security.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively for professional business purposes:

  • Conducting your requested website audit and architecture analysis.
  • Responding to technical inquiries and providing detailed project proposals.
  • Delivering contracted design, development, and growth services.
  • Maintaining the integrity, reliability, and security of our digital platforms.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data to third parties.

4. Third-Party Integrations & Processors

When delivering web solutions or processing business communications, we utilize enterprise-grade service providers that comply with strict data protection standards:

  • Payments: Stripe and Razorpay (all payment credentialing is handled directly by PCI-DSS Level 1 certified processors; we never store card numbers).
  • CRM & Inquiries: HubSpot and form dispatch providers for inquiry routing.
  • Infrastructure & Security: Cloudflare edge proxy for SSL encryption and DDoS protection.

5. Data Retention & Rights

You have the right to request access to the information we hold about you, request corrections, or request complete deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at info@weyoux.com.
