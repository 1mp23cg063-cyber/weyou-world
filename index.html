Questions

Quick answers.

What is weYou World?

weYou World is the web, app and design wing of weYou X — it designs and builds the websites and apps enterprises put in front of their customers.

Do you only work in India?

No. weYou World is based in Bengaluru and builds for clients worldwide; payment, CRM and analytics stacks are chosen per market.

What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimisation — structuring your site’s content and data so search and AI answer engines can read it and cite it accurately.

How fast can we launch?

Discovery call within one working day; a typical MVP in about 14 days; two-week sprints with live demos after that.

Do you rebuild or integrate with an existing site?

Both — new websites at world standard, or payments, CRM and analytics wired into an existing one.
