Discovery & Scoping

What happens during the initial discovery phase?

Within one working day of your inquiry, we arrange an initial discovery call. During discovery, we map the business logic, customer journeys, and integrations before writing any code. The call covers your goals, your timeline, and the tools your site needs to connect to.

We map data flows, APIs, payment infrastructure (Stripe, Razorpay, UPI), and CRM integrations (HubSpot, Salesforce) before code is written. System design and architecture are chosen for scale, leading into two-week sprints with live demos.

What should enterprises know before starting a project?

Two-Week Sprint Execution

We work in two-week sprints with live demos, with a typical MVP delivered in about 14 days. Live demos are presented every two weeks, followed by iteration from real usage.

Complete Ownership & Commercial Transparency

Upon final settlement of project invoices, the client owns all bespoke custom designs, visual assets, and application code developed specifically for the client's project. For customer communication via WhatsApp, voice, or chat, we wire enVoy into the sites and apps we build.

Inquiries & Onboarding

Frequently asked questions about starting a project

How quickly does the weYou World team respond to inquiries?

We reply within one working day to review your scope and arrange a discovery call. The initial call maps your business logic, journeys, and integrations.

What preparation is required before our initial discovery call?

The initial discovery call maps the business logic, customer journeys, and integrations before any code. Sharing your goals, timeline, and current tools is all we need to begin.

What is the typical timeframe to deliver a working MVP?

A typical MVP takes about 14 days following the discovery call. Subsequent development proceeds in two-week sprints with live demos.

Can we schedule a consultation if we are based outside India?

Yes. weYou World is based in Bengaluru and builds for clients worldwide. Payment, CRM and analytics stacks are chosen per market.

How are customer support and communication channels handled?

Project communication is available via email and WhatsApp. For customer-facing chat, voice, and WhatsApp communication on your website, we integrate enVoy.
